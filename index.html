5. Conditions & General Instructions

Command standing directives issued under BT 2026

Official Directive

Command Conditions

  • Condition 1 Entitlement Limits: Permanent sailors (MCPO, PO, LCOM) – 60 days leave in 3 breaks (3×20D). Agniveer sailors (AVR COM) – 30 days leave in 2 breaks (2×15D).
  • Condition 2 Operational Redundancy: Main and TDLR cannot both be on leave at the same time. Validated across 365 calendar days with 0 conflicts.
  • Condition 3 Station Blackouts: Strictly zero leave during Republic Day (26 Jan), Independence Day (15 Aug), Annual Inspection (15–30 Sep), Diwali (29 Oct), and Navy Day (04 Dec).

General Instructions & Authority

  • Instruction 1 All personnel are required to close liaison with the concerned maintenance agency for prompt defect rectification.
  • Instruction 2 Handing/taking over is to be completed, wherever applicable, and a completion report rendered to the issuing authority.
